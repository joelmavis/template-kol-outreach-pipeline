# Outreach priority

`outreach-priority.csv` is generated with one recommendation per candidate. Default A/P1: review and personalize; B/P2: verify fit and scope; C/P3: research first; D/P4: hold. Change these in the scoring JSON. Priorities guide review effort and do not authorize outreach. Humans review brand safety, evidence, capacity, contact preferences, and follow-up history before deciding.
