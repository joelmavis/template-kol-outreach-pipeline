How to read this

D7, D14 and D30 are cumulative snapshots from each deliverable’s publication date. Do not add them together. Spend is the cumulative amount recorded for one deliverable and is repeated across windows for context, never summed. Cost metrics are N/A when spend, outcome, attribution scope or tracking verification is missing.

Decision limits

Compare formats and creators only when the observation window, attribution source, metric definition and currency match. Small samples and incomplete attribution support hypotheses, not guaranteed growth claims.
